INTRODUCTION

Agricultural Challenges

  • Soil salinity and salt accumulation
  • Limited irrigation water
  • Poor groundwater quality

Research Gap

  • Soil salinity and groundwater quality are not adequately integrated in many existing approaches.
  • Complex predictions often lack clear, field-level guidance.

Proposed Direction

  • Integrate satellite, soil, groundwater, weather, and crop data.
  • Estimate crop yield and recommend efficient irrigation and salinity management.

PROBLEM STATEMENT

Three connected agricultural pressures.

01

Soil Salinity

Salt accumulation stresses crops and reduces yield potential.

02

Groundwater Quality

Groundwater salinity affects irrigation and leaching suitability.

03

Water Scarcity

Limited irrigation water creates difficult management choices.
