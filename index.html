03 • Agronomic Dilemma & Problem Statement

Secondary Salinization & Groundwater Crisis in Southern Punjab

Farmers face erratic canal allocations (Warabandi), shallow brackish groundwater, and habit-based over-irrigation.

3D SOIL CRUST
1. Root-Zone Salt Stress SOIL CRUSTING

Dissolved salts ($Na^+, Cl^-, SO_4^{2-}$) lower soil osmotic potential, restricting root water absorption even in flooded fields. Surface white crusts only appear after significant yield loss.

TARGET PHENOMENON
Early Subsurface $EC_e$ Inversion
3D TUBEWELL EXTRACTION
2. Unfit Tubewell Water HAZARDOUS SAR

Studies in Bahawalpur reveal >50% of sampled tubewells exceed critical irrigation thresholds ($EC_w > 1.5\,\text{dS/m}$, $SAR > 10$), silently poisoning prime wheat and cotton soil.

CRITICAL NEED
Safe Canal-Groundwater Blending
3D SATELLITE SCANNER
3. Identified Research Gap RESEARCH GAP

Past studies either use broad multispectral indices without mineral spectroscopy, leak spatial autocorrelation through random train/test splits, or publish models without accessible smallholder advisory.

ZARKHEZ AI SOLUTION
EMIT AI + FAO-56 + Android App

07 • Preprocessing & Feature Engineering

Four-Stage Preprocessing & Spectral Harmonization Pipeline

Ensuring radiometric integrity, bad-band removal, spatial resampling, and prevention of spatial autocorrelation leakage.

Pipeline Stages HARMONIZATION WORKFLOW
Stage 1: Atmospheric Calibration & Cloud Masking
EMIT L2A optimal estimation reflectance; Sentinel-2 Sen2Cor radiative transfer; filtering atmospheric absorption bands (water vapor at 1.4 & 1.9 μm).
Stage 2: Spatial Resampling & Polygon Alignment
Harmonizing 10m/20m Sentinel-2 and 60m EMIT onto a synchronized 30m grid using bilinear interpolation and cadastral field boundaries.
Stage 3: Multi-Temporal Phenology Sync
Compositing cloud-free passes over critical wheat growth windows (Vegetative, Flowering, Grain-Filling, Maturity) synced with GDD.
Stage 4: Spatial Block Partitioning (Anti-Leakage)
Partitioning data into disjoint geographic blocks so adjacent pixels never appear in both training and test sets.
Preventing Data Leakage in Spatial AI DEFENSE RIGOR

Tobler's First Law of Geography: "Near things are more related than distant things."

Standard random K-fold cross-validation randomly splits neighboring agricultural pixels, causing severe spatial autocorrelation leakage (inflating $R^2$ falsely above 0.90 while failing in real fields).

ZARKHEZ AI EVALUATION RIGOR:
1. Spatial Block Cross-Validation: Training and test folds are separated by geographic buffer zones.
2. External Holdout: Lodhran district is kept 100% blind until final validation.
Python Geospatial Stack: Rasterio • GDAL • Dask • Geopandas
